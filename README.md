## 📊 Project Overview

The housing market in DACH countries remains complex. Many users struggle to find reliable figures for managed units. This application solves that problem. It uses a bottom-up estimation method to calculate the number of professional property units. The estimate sits at 15 million homes across the region, with a range between 11 million and 19 million.

This tool functions as a central hub for market research. It pulls from open data sources and validates them through property management benchmarks.

## 📈 How It Works

The software aggregates data from regional property registers. It uses an algorithm to weigh these inputs based on historical accuracy. 

- **Data Sourcing:** The tool retrieves information from public housing databases. 
- **Filtering:** It removes duplications to ensure accurate counts.
- **Scaling:** It applies multipliers to account for private portfolios that operate under professional management.

The final output gives you a view of the total addressable market. You can export these figures to a spreadsheet for your own reports.

## 🔍 Understanding the Data

The methodology relies on a bottom-up approach. This means we look at local property management units first and aggregate them to reach a national total. This reduces errors found in top-down government reports. We look at ownership structures to define a "managed home." This includes buildings with a professional caretaker, maintenance agreements, or institutional ownership.

The range of 11 million to 19 million reflects the uncertainty in fragmented markets. In Germany, larger property portfolios drive the numbers up. In smaller markets like Austria, the estimation focuses on urban density in major cities.
